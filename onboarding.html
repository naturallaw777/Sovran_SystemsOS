Get Your Njal.la Account

Sovran_SystemsOS routes all public traffic through a private VPS tunnel, keeping your home IP completely hidden. You'll need three things from njal.la:

  1. Create a Njal.la account — njal.la accepts Bitcoin and doesn't require personal information.
  2. Purchase a domain (~$15/year) — you'll use this for Matrix, Nextcloud, and your other services. Subdomains are free, so one domain covers everything.
  3. Purchase the cheapest VPS (~$5/month) — the smallest server (512 MB RAM) is more than enough. You'll get a VPS IP address and a root password — keep them handy for the next step.

✅ No router port forwarding needed — your home IP stays completely private. All internet traffic routes through the VPS.
